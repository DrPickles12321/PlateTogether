# Roadmap

Rewritten 2026-11-27 around the FBT framework (`docs/plans/2026-11-27-fbt-framework.md`): Plate Together is
**the tool that keeps family-based treatment for teen anorexia on track**, built as five layers (record, plan,
signals, routing, screens) driven by a program × phase engine. Read that doc first; this one only orders the work.

Each step is one PR: small, tested, and checked on a phone and a desktop before merge. **Decision** marks a step
that needs a human (founders or a clinician) first. **Can start now** marks steps with no open decision.

The claim we are building towards: **more families on track by session 4.**

## Phase A: check and decide (no code)

1. **Talk to 3 to 5 FBT clinicians and 3 to 5 parents** with `docs/clinical/2026-11-27-fbt-interview-guide.md`.
   It tests the four things that set us apart: early response, hard meal mode, the session guide, team roles.
2. ~~Storage~~ **Decided 2026-11-27:** Firestore, with sign-in upgraded to Identity Platform; both are on
   Google's BAA list, Realtime Database and plain Firebase Auth are not (framework section 8). A human enables
   Firestore and the Identity Platform upgrade in the console when phase B is ready to switch over.
3. **Clinician reviews:** the wording review and the metrics draft (already written).
4. **Working defaults accepted 2026-11-27, a clinician confirms in the interviews:** weight recorded by
   clinicians (parents see it only if the clinician says so, the teen never); the teen's role per phase; who
   gets which alert (framework section 9). Team-only notes: yes, hidden from the family's screens but not secret
   (framework section 7).
5. **Billing question:** ask a clinic's billing person whether remote therapeutic monitoring codes could apply,
   and what data they need (believed 16 of 30 days; unverified).
6. **Pitch one-pager:** the problem with its evidence (framework section 2), what sets us apart, what is built,
   and the pilot plan. Fake data and screenshots only.

## Phase B: the foundation, one family and an event record

Everything after this builds on it. See framework section 7 for the shape, invariants and migration.

1. **Program × phase config:** **Done.** `src/lib/programs.js` (one program, `anorexia-fbt`, phases 1 to 3 and
   maintenance). Phase labels and the parent nutrition-number default now come from it.
2. **Rules first:** ~~Firestore rules for `families/` with emulator tests~~ **Done:** `firestore.rules`, 31 tests
   in `tests/firestore/` (`npm run test:firestore`). Not deployed and not used by the app until B5.
3. **Event record and read model:** **Done.** `src/lib/events.js` (16 tests) builds and checks events, orders
   them by server time, works out the latest state per meal (with who logged it, when, and how often the
   outcome changed), gives the screens today's data shapes, and converts old data for the migration. Its event
   types are tested against `firestore.rules`.
4. **Migration script** from Realtime Database to Firestore: dry run, then `--apply`; old meal slots become
   events "by the original parent, time unknown"; the old data stays read-only until a human deletes it.
   Needs B2, B3.
5. **Switch the app** to read and write `families/`. Checked on the emulators with fake families, then a human
   deploys rules and runs the migration.
6. **Several caregivers:** invites, both parents logging, separate households. Needs B5.
7. **Team roles:** therapist (lead), dietitian, pediatrician, psychiatrist, coordinator; plan items carry an
   owner role and a version history. Needs B5 and a clinician check of the roles
   (`docs/plans/2026-11-27-care-team.md`).

## Phase C: hard meal mode (the parent at the table)

1. **Calm screen:** "This meal is hard" opens the clinician's lines for this child one at a time, with stay-at-
   the-table guidance. Uses today's care plan. **Can start now.**
2. **Debrief:** three skippable taps after a hard meal; shown in the session summary. **Can start now** (stored
   as today's meal data until B5, then as events).
3. **Push notifications** (Firebase Cloud Messaging, free) and **tag in** the other caregiver. Needs B6.
4. **Works offline:** installable web app with an offline queue for logs. **Can start now.**

## Phase D: early response

1. **Clinic measurements:** clinician-only area for weight and vitals entered at visits; the clinician chooses
   whether parents see weight; the teen never does. Needs B5.
2. **Early-response signal** (`src/lib/signals/earlyResponse.js`, tests): on track or not by session 4, defaults
   from the research, thresholds editable by the clinician, evidence shown. No clinical suggestions.
3. **In the family header and session summary,** clinician screens only.

## Phase E: phases, the session guide and the teen

1. **Phase changes as events,** with a short checklist the clinician confirms. Builds on today's phase picker.
2. **Session guide per phase:** our own short summaries of what each phase and session covers, written with a
   clinician (no manual text copied), shown in the session summary. **Decision:** content, with a clinician.
3. **Teen account, phase-gated:** nothing about food in phase 1; propose-and-approve in phase 2 (the parent
   approves or declines); own check-ins after. Never weight, numbers or meal logs. Needs B7.
4. **Maintenance mode** after phase 3 or discharge: lighter logging, the clinician's warning-sign list.

## Phase F: signals and routing

1. **Clinician statistics module** (`src/lib/clinicianStats.js`) from the agreed metric list. Needs A3.
2. **Caregiver check-in:** two questions a week each; strain trend for the clinician.
3. **Parents in sync:** gaps by caregiver, meal or household.
4. **Alert routing with fallbacks:** each signal to its owning role, reminders if nobody acknowledges, via a
   Worker cron trigger (free tier). Needs B7.
5. **Meal reminders** when a meal window passes with nothing logged. Text never includes meal content.
6. **Relapse watch** in phase 2, 3 and maintenance: refusals creeping back up, routed to the therapist.
7. **Role-shaped first screens** and "since you last saw them" for every team member.

## Phase G: the gate before real families

Required by AGENTS.md and `COMPLIANCE.md`. **No real patient data before this phase is done.**

1. Sign the Google Cloud / Firebase BAA and confirm every service used is covered.
2. A BAA with the model provider for any AI feature, or no AI features with real data.
3. De-identify anything sent to a model (no names, emails, family codes; relative dates), in the Worker, tested.
4. Audit log of clinician and assistant access: who, which family, when; no meal content in the log.
5. The rest of the `COMPLIANCE.md` list, item by item.

## Phase H: pilot

1. One or two clinics, a handful of consenting families; read the consent with a clinician.
2. Measure: share of families on track by session 4, caregiver strain over time, clinician minutes per family,
   how often hard meal mode and tag-in are used, and what clinicians say.
3. Fix what the pilot shows before adding anything new.

## Later

- **Food photo:** a photo suggests food names, the parent confirms, the photo is discarded. Never calories or
  portions from the photo. After phase G, through the Worker.
- **Clinician assistant:** an existing model behind the Worker that takes numbers only from the signals layer
  and knowledge only from vetted, licensed clinical sources, with an evaluation set (50 to 100 clinician
  questions plus safety cases) it must pass first; read-only pilot; fine-tuning only if the evaluation shows a
  gap. Needs phase G.
- **More programs** (for example ARFID): a new config block plus clinician-written content.
