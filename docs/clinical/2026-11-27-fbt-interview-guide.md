# Interview guide: FBT clinicians and parents

**Purpose:** check the FBT framework (`docs/plans/2026-11-27-fbt-framework.md`) before building it. Roadmap
phase A1. Aim for 3 to 5 FBT clinicians (therapists first, a dietitian and a pediatrician if possible) and 3 to 5
parents who have been through FBT.

**Time:** 30 to 40 minutes each. **Format:** video or in person, one interviewer and one note-taker.

## Ground rules

- Ask about what they **did last time**, not what they would do. Past behaviour is better evidence than opinions.
- Don't pitch. Show the app only at the end, if at all, and only with fake data.
- **No patient details in notes.** No names, dates of birth, places or anything that identifies a family. Write
  "a 14-year-old in phase 1", not more. Parents may tell painful stories; let them stop at any time.
- If a parent mentions a current safety risk (self-harm, fainting, not eating for a day or more), stop and point
  them to their care team or emergency services. We are not providing care.
- Get verbal consent to take notes; offer to share what we learn.

---

## Clinicians

**Warm-up (5 min)**
1. What is your role, and roughly how many families in FBT do you see at once?
2. Which formats do you use: standard FBT, separated, telehealth, day program?

**Between sessions (problem 1)**
3. Think of the last family you saw. How did you find out how the week's meals went?
4. What did you wish you had known that you only found out later?
5. What do families bring today (food records, notes, apps)? What do you actually use?

**Early response (problem 3)**
6. How do you decide in the first month whether a family is on track? What do you look at?
7. When a family isn't on track by around session 4, what do you change?
8. If an app showed "not yet on track" from clinic weights, would you trust it? What would it need to show?
9. Weight: do you share it with parents? Would you enter it in an app if it stayed clinician-only by default?

**At the table (problems 2 and 5)**
10. What do parents most often get wrong at a hard meal? What do you tell them to do?
11. Would you write a few lines for a parent to read during a hard meal? How short would they need to be?
12. How often do two caregivers do things differently? How do you notice?

**The team (problem 4)**
13. Who else is involved in a typical family's care? How do you share information with them today?
14. When did the family last get mixed messages from different team members? What happened?
15. Who should be told about what, and how fast? (Give examples: a refused dinner, two refused days, fainting.)

**Phases and transitions (problem 6)**
16. How do you decide to move a family from phase 1 to phase 2? What goes wrong at that point?
17. After treatment ends, how do you know if a family is slipping?
18. What should the teen be able to see or do in an app, by phase? What should they never see?

**Delivering FBT (problems 7 and 9)**
19. How closely do you follow the manual? Where do you adapt, and why?
20. Would a short guide per phase or session, built into the family's weekly summary, help you or a less
    experienced colleague? What would make it annoying?

**Practicalities**
21. How much time per family per week could you give to reviewing an app? What would make it worth it?
22. Does your clinic bill for remote monitoring today? Who would know?

**Close (5 min)**
23. If you could change one thing about how FBT works between sessions, what would it be?
24. Can we show you a prototype in a few weeks? Would you review two short drafts (wording, metrics)?

## Parents

**Warm-up**
1. Without names, how old was your child, and how long ago did FBT start?
2. Who did the meals at home? One parent, both, other family?

**At the table**
3. Tell me about the last hard meal you remember. What happened, step by step?
4. In that moment, what did you need? Did you have it?
5. Did you call or message anyone? Who, and did it help?
6. If your phone had shown three short lines from your therapist right then, would you have looked at it?

**Two caregivers**
7. How did you and the other caregiver stay on the same page? Where did it break down?
8. If you could tap once to ask the other parent to come and help, would you use it? When not?

**Sessions and the team**
9. How did you remember what happened in the week by the time of the session?
10. Did you ever get different advice from different people on the team? What happened?

**Logging**
11. Did you keep any food records? What made it easy or hard?
12. How many seconds would you spend logging a meal on a normal day? On a hard day?
13. Is there anything you would not want written down or shared with the clinician?

**Your child**
14. What would your child have thought about an app? What would have made it worse for them?
15. When control started going back to your child, how did that go? What would have helped?

**Close**
16. If you could change one thing about the support you had between sessions, what would it be?

---

## After each interview

Write a half-page summary in a private, shared place (not this repo): role, the three strongest quotes
(anonymous), and for each framework idea (early response, hard meal mode, session guide, team roles, teen role,
weight) mark **supports / mixed / against**, with the reason. After five interviews, update the framework doc's
open decisions and the roadmap.
